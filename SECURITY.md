# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report vulnerabilities **privately** through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting: open the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability** ([direct link](https://github.com/simpleenough77-fb/spoolspot/security/advisories/new)). Do not open a public issue or pull request for a vulnerability, and do not include real secrets or personal data in a report.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, the version or commit, and the impact you expect.

## What to expect

- Acknowledgement within **3 business days**.
- Triage (severity and next step) within **7 days**.
- We coordinate a fix and a disclosure date with you and credit you if you wish.

These are targets, not guarantees. SpoolSpot is a volunteer project.

## Scope

In scope: this repository's code, its CI configuration, and the tag resolver and hosted service once they exist. Out of scope: third-party services, social engineering, denial-of-service testing, and findings that need a compromised device.

## Supported versions

Only the latest release and `main` receive security fixes while the project is pre-1.0.

## Safe harbor

Good-faith research that follows this policy and avoids privacy violations and service disruption is welcome. This policy is not legal advice or a legal commitment.

A `security.txt` will be published with the hosted service.
